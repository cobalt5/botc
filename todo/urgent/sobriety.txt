Constrain Sober
===============

PROBLEM
Nothing in situations.md constrains Sober(A, t). Every claim row has the form
    Sober(A, ...) and Align(A, Good, ...) -> ...
so in every world a claim can be switched off by making its player not
sober. On their own, the claims rule out no worlds (see "With the current
rules only" in example.md).

CHANGE (situations.md)
Add a predicate for poisoning (or reuse Chose) and an invariant along the
lines of:
    Sober(A, (t, k)) <-> not Role(A, Drunk, (t, k))
                         and not Poisoned(A, (t, k))
    Poisoned(A, (t, k)) for k after the Poisoner acts on night t, when a
    sober Poisoner X has Chose(X, A, (t, k_Poisoner)), lasting until the
    Poisoner acts again (or dies).
List every cause of drunkenness and poisoning on the script, since the
"<->" means a player is sober unless one of them applies. On Trouble
Brewing: the Drunk and the Poisoner. On other scripts, more roles cause
this (e.g. Sailor, Innkeeper, Courtier, No Dashii, Vortox).
