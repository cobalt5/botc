Connect good players' claims to their roles
===========================================

PROBLEM
The claim rows only require Sober and Good. Nothing connects a claim to
Role(A, claimed role), so the rules allow a Good player to claim a role
they don't have. example.md has to assume this rule to be solvable
("Good players claim truthfully").

CHANGE (situations.md)
Add a Claims(A, R) predicate and an invariant along the lines of:
    Align(A, Good, (1, 0)) and Claims(A, R)
        -> Role(A, R, (1, 0)) or (Role(A, Drunk, (1, 0)) and R in T
                                   and not InPlay(R))
The Drunk needs its own case: it believes it is a Townsfolk that is not
in play. Decide whether this is an invariant (always assumed) or a
per-game assumption, since real good players can bluff.
