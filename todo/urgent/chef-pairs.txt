Fix the Chef row to count every adjacent pair
=============================================

PROBLEM
The Chef row in situations.md counts only "pairs of neighbours (B, C) not
including the chef". The real Chef counts every pair of adjacent evil
players, including pairs with the Chef in them.

It made no difference in example.md, because a Good, sober Chef never
registers as evil, so no pair with the Chef in it counts. It is still
wrong as a statement of the ability. It gives wrong answers whenever the
Chef can register as evil, for example an evil player claiming Chef, or a
Good Chef whose alignment has changed.

CHANGE (situations.md, "A claims Chef, sees N")
Replace "for all pairs of neighbours (B, C) not including the chef" with
"for all pairs of adjacent players (B, C)". Optionally write the count
formally:
    sum over adjacent (B, C) of
        [Registers(B, Evil, (1, k_Chef)) and Registers(C, Evil, (1, k_Chef))] = N
