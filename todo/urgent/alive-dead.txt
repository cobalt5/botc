Add a Dead/Alive predicate
==========================

PROBLEM
There is no predicate for whether a player is alive. In example.md these
were worked out by hand:
  - the Empath's "nearest alive neighbours" on night 2 (D executed day 1,
    C killed at (2, 30))
  - the Ravenkeeper only waking because C died at night
  - D not being the Imp because the game continued after day 1

CHANGE (situations.md)
Add Alive(A, t) and Died(A, t), plus rules linking them:
    Executed(A, t) -> not Alive(A, (t+1, 1))
    death at (t, k) -> not Alive(A, next(t, k))
    not Alive stays not Alive (until revived, which Trouble Brewing never does)
Then rewrite the Empath row's "nearest alive neighbours" as a formal
condition, and condition the Ravenkeeper row on Died(A, (t, k)) for some
k < k_Ravenkeeper. Needs next() from role-persistence.txt.
